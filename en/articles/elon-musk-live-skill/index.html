Live Skill Package / First Principles

The Live Elon Musk Skill

A first-principles advisor for founders, managers, and owners. Updated automatically by Codex once a day. Public data is pulled in and structured as live-data so this Skill stays close to the latest context. News items serve as reference only; the core Skill itself still requires human review before any official update.

The positioning of this Skill is clear: it distills Elon Musk's characteristic thinking patterns into a first-principles advisor that AI can call upon. Its purpose is thinking practice, decision-making cross-examination, and data tracking. News content lives in the reference layer only.

Core First Principles

Break a problem down to its most fundamental facts, constraints, costs, and physical or business conditions, then rebuild the solution from scratch.

Use Case Decision Cross-Examination

When you are stuck on strategy, product, organization, or resource allocation, let AI surface the real constraints and blind spots by asking the right questions first.

Live Data Daily Updates

Public data is pulled every day and sorted into four categories: direct personal content, first-hand company sources, news reports, and news commentary.

How founders, managers, and owners can use it

This Skill is most effective right before a high-stakes decision. It unpacks your question and forces you to see the assumptions underneath, temporarily setting aside polished, aphorism-style answers.

Founder Founders: stress-test product and market assumptions

Use it to examine a new product, service, or course. It will press you: Is this pain point a verified fact, or do you just hope it is? What workarounds are people using right now? Are you solving a real need, or packaging a feature?

Manager Managers: diagnose team bottlenecks

Use it to examine team processes, delivery speed, and communication overhead. It will press you: Is the real blocker headcount, information flow, authority, tooling, or an unclear goal?

Owner Owners: scrutinize resource allocation

Use it to decide whether to launch a new line, kill an old project, or invest in automation. It will press you: What is the minimum verifiable action? If the budget were cut to one third, which single thing would you still do?

Operator Operators: question the necessity of routine work

Use it to identify which processes AI can absorb and which steps still require human judgment. It will press you: Is this step creating value, or maintaining a habit?

How a first-principles advisor cross-examines you

The core capability I want this Skill to have is the ability to ask back. It does not rush to an answer; it first dismantles the question down to a more fundamental level.

  • In this problem, which parts have you personally verified as facts? Which parts are assumptions that have simply been repeated long enough by the market, your team, or yourself to feel true?
  • If you removed everything you currently do and kept only the goal and its constraints, what is the minimum viable path forward?
  • What are you most trying to protect right now: customer value, team morale, existing processes, or sunk costs you have already committed?
  • If this approach fails, which link breaks first? Demand, cost, supply, delivery, trust, or speed?
  • Are you looking for a more elegant answer, or for a small experiment that reality can test?
I call it the soul test.Its purpose is to help you see the blind spots in a problem clearly. Much of the time, the most valuable thing is not the answer itself but the question that makes you stop and rethink.

The Codex daily auto-update mechanism

This repo contains live-data. Codex runs an automatic update once a day, pulling in new public data. The daily task updates only the data layer; it does not modify the core Skill file directly.

01 Pull public data

Google News RSS, company and official sources, and publicly visible content from Elon Musk's public X profile.

02 Categorize sources

Sorted into direct personal content, first-hand company sources, news reports, and news commentary so they are never mixed together.

03 Generate daily digest

A digest and raw JSONL are produced each day, preserving both the original data and summaries for easy reference.

04 Monthly human consolidation

A full consolidation happens once a month. Updating the core Skill requires passing a promotion gate so daily news cannot overwrite the persona's core directly.

Category Meaning How it is used
musk_direct Publicly visible content from Elon Musk himself, including what is available on his public X profile. Treated as primary first-person context, but source timestamps and original links are always preserved.
company_primary First-hand company or official sources such as announcements, blog posts, and press releases. Used to fill in company decisions, product updates, and technical progress.
news_report Factual reporting from news outlets. Reference only. Cannot be treated as Musk's personal position or as a Skill conclusion.
news_commentary Opinion pieces, analyses, and perspective-driven articles. Labeled clearly as commentary, used to observe external interpretations, not to make value judgments.
Disclaimer on news dataNews and commentary may carry a particular angle, contain disputed claims, or be out of date. The daily update flags such content as reference material, reminding readers that it represents media coverage and does not reflect Elon Musk's own statements or Coach Jiang's endorsement.
Why SKILL.md is not updated every dayThe core Skill file shapes how AI thinks. If it were pushed by daily news, short-term events would easily end up written into the persona's core. Daily tasks therefore update only live-data; a monthly consolidation follows, with significant content entering a review queue and passing a promotion gate before the core Skill is officially updated.

How to get the most out of this Skill

Treat it as a first-principles advisor and bring your real problem to it directly. The more specific you are, the more useful it becomes.

Example promptI want to launch an AI workflow course aimed at managers in small and medium-sized businesses. Please use first principles to break down this product. Do not write marketing copy yet. Instead, ask me the ten questions I most need to clarify, and point out which assumptions I may be taking for granted.

Whether you are working on a product, course, service, team, or company decision, this Skill can help you decompose the problem, surface the assumptions, and turn the next step into a verifiable action.
