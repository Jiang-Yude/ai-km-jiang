AI-Ready site · 4O optimization

Easy to find, read and use, for people and for AI

This site isn't built for human readers alone. From search engines to AI assistants, I want every kind of reader to find me, understand me and put me to use. The whole method is public.

SEO

Search engines can find it

Sitemap, structured data, clean URLs. Google can find it and knows what each page is about.

AIO (incl. AEO)

AI summaries can read it

A site summary written for AI (llms.txt). ChatGPT and Claude read it and understand in one pass who I am and what I do.

GEO

Generative engines will cite it

Content shaped so AI can pick it up and retell it. When someone asks an AI about these topics, I'm more likely to come up.

AXO

AI agents read the data directly

Services, positioning and the full site index are published as open data files. AI agents don't guess and don't need a key. They just read.

Control layer

The layer that decides how the whole thing runs

AGENTS.md, skill retrieval, the tag dictionary, hooks, loops and review rules.

See how I did it ⌄

1. From a site for humans to a site both humans and AI can read

Websites used to serve one kind of reader: people. These days the thing looking something up for you, or helping you decide, is often an AI. So I upgraded this site until both kinds of reader could follow it.

2. Two layers

The first layer is static data files: the full site index site-index.json, my positioning profile.json, my services offers.json, and a summary written for AI llms.txt. Fixed URLs, open formats. Paste one into any AI and it can read it.

The second layer is dynamic endpoints: small services that compute in real time, like the view counter.

(Every filename below is a live link. Click one and you'll see exactly what it looks like.)

3. Why there's no API key

This data exists so people and AI can find me. It was never meant to be gated or charged for. So I deliberately made it public, no signup, read it straight. When people ask how to apply for a key, my answer is: you don't. Just read the URL.

4. One source of truth per fact

For things like positioning and services I maintain a single source, and the rest of the site updates from it automatically. That way what a person sees and what an AI reads are always the same version, with no two stories that fail to line up.
