Four Key Terms, in Plain English

  • Agent: An AI that lives on your computer and works directly with your files. It renames files, converts formats, and organizes folders for you.
  • Codex / Claude Code: Two brands of Agent. The courses use these two primarily; install either one and you're ready.
  • Knowledge Base: Your personal work manual, written for AI. Every time AI starts a task, it reads this first, which is how it remembers your standards.
  • Skills: An instruction set that teaches AI to do one specific thing well. The knowledge base is the brain, Skills are the manual, and your AI employee is the one who follows through.
